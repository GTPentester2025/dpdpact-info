# Org Plan for dpdpact.info

## Mission
Build `dpdpact.info` into a trustworthy, SEO-driven DPDP Act resource and lead-generation business.

## Non-negotiables
- No deceptive claims
- No pretending to be lawyers if legal advice is not being provided
- Human review for legal/compliance-sensitive content
- Transparent monetization
- Security-first operations

## Team Structure

### 1) General Manager / PM
**Owns:** priorities, roadmap, budget, publishing cadence, cross-team coordination.

**Responsibilities:**
- Maintain backlog
- Decide weekly priorities
- Track KPIs
- Approve experiments
- Coordinate launches

### 2) Product + Tech Lead
**Owns:** architecture, code quality, deployment standards, stack choices.

**Responsibilities:**
- Set project structure
- Review implementation plans
- Keep delivery simple and maintainable
- Ensure analytics and SEO basics are wired in

### 3) Frontend / UX Team
**Owns:** homepage, landing pages, conversion flows, user clarity.

**Responsibilities:**
- Design pages for founders, DPOs, legal teams, and consultants
- Improve readability and trust signals
- Optimize CTAs and lead forms

### 4) Content / SEO Team
**Owns:** keyword research, briefs, publishing calendar, internal linking.

**Responsibilities:**
- Build topic clusters around DPDP compliance
- Produce briefs for pages/articles
- Update content as the law and guidance evolve
- Track rankings and CTR

### 5) Editorial / Fact-Check Team
**Owns:** accuracy, tone, citation hygiene, disclaimers.

**Responsibilities:**
- Check claims against source material
- Distinguish informational content from legal advice
- Maintain style guide

### 6) Security / Ops Team
**Owns:** hardening, secrets, backups, updates, monitoring.

**Responsibilities:**
- Require non-root deployment
- Configure firewall, SSH keys, fail2ban, updates, backups
- Review headers, TLS, and runtime security

### 7) Growth / Monetization Team
**Owns:** revenue experiments, analytics, funnels, partnerships.

**Responsibilities:**
- Test lead magnets
- Add newsletter and template offers
- Explore privacy/compliance SaaS affiliates
- Build sponsor and partner pages

## Operating Rhythm

### Daily
- Check site uptime and errors
- Review content tasks in progress
- Review analytics snapshots

### Weekly
- Publish 2-5 useful pieces of content
- Update internal links
- Review top pages and drop-offs
- Plan one monetization experiment

### Monthly
- Refresh underperforming content
- Compare organic growth and revenue metrics
- Review server security and dependencies

## KPI Dashboard
- Organic clicks
- Impressions
- CTR
- Newsletter signups
- Lead form conversions
- Template sales
- Revenue per 1,000 visits
- Top landing pages by conversion

## Budget-first Principles
- Start static/simple, avoid heavy infra
- Use one clean stack
- Reuse templates and layouts
- Focus on high-intent pages first
- Automate drafts, not final publishing decisions
