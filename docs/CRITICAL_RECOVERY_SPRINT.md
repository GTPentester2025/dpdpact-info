# Critical Recovery Sprint

## Mission
Make `dpdpact.info` look materially more credible, more substantial, and more commercially intentional in a single visible sprint.

## Immediate Priorities
- Expand page depth and count meaningfully
- Improve guide consistency and perceived professionalism
- Strengthen SEO architecture and internal linking
- Shift toward source-backed, legitimate content standards
- Improve premium feel across key site pages

## Visible Outcomes Expected This Sprint
- Major increase in meaningful page inventory
- Stronger hub pages and navigation
- Better CTA system and lead asset positioning
- More consistent guide structure
- Better subject coverage for founders, startups, SaaS, e-commerce, and operators

## Constraints
- Keep stack static/simple
- Avoid thin or spammy pages
- Avoid pretending legal authority
- Prioritize visible credibility over gimmicks
