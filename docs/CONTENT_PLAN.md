# Content Plan for dpdpact.info

## Positioning
`dpdpact.info` should become the most practical plain-English resource hub for India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, especially for founders, startups, SaaS teams, agencies, and operators.

## Core Audiences
- Founders and startup operators
- SaaS teams
- E-commerce businesses
- Agencies/service providers
- Compliance and legal-adjacent teams

## Content Buckets

### 1. High-intent evergreen pages
These pages target users actively searching for compliance help.
- What is the DPDP Act?
- DPDP compliance checklist
- DPDP vs GDPR
- Privacy notice template
- Consent requirements under DPDP
- Data principal rights explained
- Children’s data rules
- DPDP penalties explained

### 2. Operational implementation guides
These convert search traffic into leads or template downloads.
- How to map personal data in your startup
- How to audit your signup and consent flows
- How to build a data retention checklist
- How to review vendors and processors
- How to respond to deletion/data access requests

### 3. Industry-specific pages
These improve commercial intent.
- DPDP compliance for SaaS
- DPDP compliance for e-commerce
- DPDP compliance for agencies
- DPDP compliance for health/edtech/fintech startups

### 4. Trust and conversion pages
- About
- Contact
- Newsletter
- Templates
- Consulting/Advisory (future)
- Sponsor/Partner page (future)

## Internal Linking Strategy
- Link every article back to the homepage and at least 2 related guides
- Use template/newsletter pages as CTA destinations
- Add “related guides” blocks on every article
- Build category hubs later

## Conversion Strategy
- Newsletter signup CTA on homepage and guides
- Template download CTA from checklist/compliance pages
- Contact CTA for consulting/partnership inquiries
- Add a lightweight lead capture offer: “DPDP starter checklist PDF”
