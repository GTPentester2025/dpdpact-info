Privacy Notice Review Sheet

Use this sheet to compare what your business tells users against what your products, teams, and vendors actually do.

Review prompts

  1. What personal data categories are mentioned?
  2. Do the purposes described match real workflows?
  3. Do product, support, CRM, and marketing tools create gaps between notice and reality?
  4. Who owns updates when data practices change?

Use with the privacy notice checklist and data mapping guide.
