Consent Flow Review Worksheet

Use this worksheet to review how consent is requested, what users are told, and what happens after collection.

Worksheet prompts

  1. Where is the consent request shown?
  2. What exact language is shown to users?
  3. What purposes are tied to the flow?
  4. Can the team show what users saw at the time of collection?

Use with the consent guide, lawful uses page, and privacy notice checklist.
