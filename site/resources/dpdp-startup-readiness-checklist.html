DPDP Startup Readiness Checklist

A founder-friendly starter resource for reviewing what data your business collects, where risk tends to hide, and what to tighten first.

What this checklist helps you review

  • Data collection visibility
  • Consent and notice quality
  • Retention and deletion gaps
  • Ownership for privacy-related tasks
  • Vendor and workflow blind spots

This page can evolve into a true downloadable asset or email-gated lead magnet in the next iteration.

Quick checklist

  1. Do we know what personal data we collect?
  2. Can we explain why we collect it?
  3. Do our notices and consent flows match reality?
  4. Do we know where data is stored and for how long?
  5. Does someone own privacy-related operational follow-up?
