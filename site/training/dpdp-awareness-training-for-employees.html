DPDP Awareness Training for Employees

Most teams do not need abstract lectures. They need short training that explains what data they touch, what mistakes create risk, and what to escalate.

What good training should cover

  • What counts as personal data
  • Basic notice/consent awareness
  • Rights and grievance routing
  • Vendor/tool awareness
  • Deletion, retention, and escalation basics

Commercial angle

This page outlines a practical awareness-training direction for real teams and can support workshop or internal enablement use cases.
