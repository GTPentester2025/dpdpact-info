DPDP certification and training

Teams looking for DPDP learning options should distinguish clearly between formal certifications, certificate courses, workshops, and internal awareness training.

What to look for

  • Clear scope and audience
  • Practical implementation value
  • India relevance where needed
  • Honest claims about what is and is not “official” certification

Better framing

For many startups, awareness training and practical workshops are more useful than prestige-signaling courses with little workflow relevance.
