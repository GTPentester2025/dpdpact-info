DPDP for E-commerce Businesses

Audience: founders, e-commerce ops, growth teams, customer teams · Last reviewed: March 2026

E-commerce teams touch customer profiles, shipping data, support records, marketing permissions, transaction-related information, and retention problems across multiple tools.

Where risk usually shows up

  • Checkout and account creation flows
  • Email/SMS lifecycle marketing
  • Customer support and logistics tooling
  • Retention and deletion inconsistencies across systems

Practical first review

  1. Review what data is collected at checkout and signup
  2. Check how marketing permissions are handled
  3. Map where customer data travels after purchase
  4. Review retention habits across support, CRM, and logistics systems
