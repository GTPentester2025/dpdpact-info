Role Guide

DPDP for Product Teams

Audience: PMs, product ops, founders · Last reviewed: March 2026

Product teams control collection points, user flows, notices, consent experience, and many of the changes that create privacy drift over time.

What product teams should review

  1. What data is collected at each step
  2. What users are told when it is collected
  3. What changes downstream after release
  4. What should be retired, minimized, or documented better
