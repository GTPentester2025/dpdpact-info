DPDP Compliance Checklist

Audience: founders, ops, product, compliance-minded teams · Last reviewed: March 2026

This checklist is the simplest useful starting point for reviewing whether your business has the basic ingredients of practical DPDP readiness.

Checklist

  1. Map what personal data you collect
  2. Document why you collect it
  3. Review notices and consent points
  4. Check rights and grievance handling workflows
  5. Review retention and deletion practices
  6. Review vendors and processors
  7. Assign internal ownership

What this usually reveals

Most teams discover gaps in form design, notice language, deletion logic, vendor visibility, and internal ownership long before they discover exotic legal issues.

Best next move

Use this page as a first-pass operational audit, then move into the linked deeper guides for each weak area.
