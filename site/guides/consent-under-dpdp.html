Consent Under the DPDP Act

Consent is not just a checkbox problem. Teams need to think about clarity, timing, purpose, and how the user experience actually behaves in practice.

What teams usually get wrong

  • Bundling too many purposes together
  • Using vague or overloaded language
  • Making consent technically present but operationally messy
  • Not reviewing downstream data use after collection

Practical review questions

  1. Is the user told what data is collected and why?
  2. Is consent understandable without lawyer translation?
  3. Does the product team know what happens after collection?
  4. Can the organization demonstrate what users were shown?

Informational only. Validate operational/legal details for business-critical workflows.
