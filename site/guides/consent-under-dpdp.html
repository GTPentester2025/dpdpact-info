Consent Under DPDP

Audience: founders, product, growth, ops · Last reviewed: March 2026

Consent is not just a checkbox problem. It is a workflow design problem involving language, timing, purpose, and what the business really does after collection.

Where teams get it wrong

  • Bundling too many purposes together
  • Using vague or overloaded language
  • Letting product behavior drift away from what users were shown
  • Failing to log what was actually presented

What to review

  1. Signup flows
  2. Lead-gen forms
  3. Email/SMS capture points
  4. Checkout and lifecycle communications
