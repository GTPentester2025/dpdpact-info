DPDP for Edtech

Audience: founders, edtech ops, product teams · Last reviewed: March 2026

Edtech teams should take extra care where products touch student data, parent interactions, school workflows, and any minor-related or sensitive educational context.

What to review first

  1. Account creation and student/parent flows
  2. Notice and consent language
  3. Data visibility across classroom/admin systems
  4. Children’s data implications and escalation points
