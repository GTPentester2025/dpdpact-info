Key DPDP Terms Explained

Audience: founders, operators, product teams · Last reviewed: March 2026

Most teams lose time because they read legal terms without translating them into workflow language.

Terms worth understanding early

  • Personal data
  • Data principal
  • Data fiduciary
  • Consent
  • Children’s data
  • Grievance handling

Practical takeaway

Teams should treat these as operating terms, not just legal vocabulary. If nobody internally can explain them in plain English, implementation will be messy.
