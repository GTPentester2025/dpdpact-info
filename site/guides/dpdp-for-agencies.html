DPDP for Agencies

Audience: agencies, service providers, founders · Last reviewed: March 2026

Agencies often touch lead-gen data, CRM data, campaign data, support workflows, and client systems without fully mapping the privacy consequences of that access.

What agencies should review

  • What client or end-user data they can access
  • Which subcontractors or tools also touch that data
  • What notices and consent assumptions sit upstream
  • How responsibility is split across client and provider workflows
