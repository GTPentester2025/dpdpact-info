Data Retention and Deletion Checklist

Last reviewed: March 2026 · Informational only, not legal advice

Retention problems usually come from neglect, not evil. Teams keep too much because nobody owned cleanup.

Review checklist

  1. List core systems that store personal data
  2. Check whether retention periods exist anywhere
  3. Review whether deleted users remain in downstream systems
  4. Document where manual cleanup is still required

Common mistakes

  • Assuming deletion happens automatically
  • Forgetting analytics, support, and CRM systems
  • Letting backups and exports become invisible shadow copies
