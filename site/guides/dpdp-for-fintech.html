DPDP for Fintech

Audience: founders, fintech ops, compliance-minded teams · Last reviewed: March 2026

Fintech teams often deal with onboarding, KYC-adjacent flows, customer support, payments, vendors, and sensitive workflow sprawl. That raises the cost of sloppy data handling fast.

Where risk shows up

  • Onboarding and identity-related flows
  • Support, CRM, and collections-style workflows
  • Vendors and third-party data access
  • Retention assumptions across customer systems
