What is the DPDP Act?

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act is India’s framework for governing how digital personal data is collected, processed, and protected.

Why it matters

If your business collects user or customer data, privacy obligations are becoming operational, not optional.

Who should care

  • Startups
  • SaaS companies
  • E-commerce businesses
  • Agencies and service providers
