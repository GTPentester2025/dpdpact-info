Right to Nominate Under DPDP

Audience: compliance-minded teams, founders, ops · Last reviewed: March 2026

This is one of the more DPDP-specific rights topics. Even if many businesses rarely encounter it day-to-day, it matters as part of a complete rights-handling framework.

Why it matters

Teams should think about what records, verification steps, and escalation logic would be needed if nomination-related handling becomes relevant in practice.

Operational questions

  • What records need to exist?
  • How would verification be handled?
  • Who owns edge-case review?
  • What abuse-prevention steps are reasonable?
