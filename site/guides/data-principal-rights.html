Data Principal Rights Explained

If users have rights, your team needs workflows. Otherwise privacy rights become support chaos.

What this means in practice

  • Someone needs to receive requests
  • Someone needs to verify and route them
  • Someone needs to know where relevant data lives
  • Someone needs to respond within a defined process

Operational takeaway

Founders should treat privacy rights handling like an internal SOP, not a legal footnote.
