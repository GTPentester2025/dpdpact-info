DPDP for Startups

Most startups do not need a giant privacy bureaucracy. They do need visibility into what data they collect, why they collect it, and how the product and ops teams actually use it.

Priority order for startups

  1. Map user/customer data
  2. Clean up consent and notice flows
  3. Review retention habits
  4. Assign ownership for requests and privacy operations
  5. Review vendors and processors

The goal is not perfection theater. The goal is competent, defensible operations.
