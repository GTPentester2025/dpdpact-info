How to Handle Deletion Requests

Audience: ops, support, product, founders · Last reviewed: March 2026

Deletion rights become messy when teams do not know where data actually lives.

What a practical workflow needs

  1. One owner for intake
  2. Clear identity verification step
  3. System mapping for where data lives
  4. A response process and internal checklist

Common failure points

  • Support teams guessing instead of following SOPs
  • Analytics/CRM systems being forgotten
  • Manual work with no tracking trail
