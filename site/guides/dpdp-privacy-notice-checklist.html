DPDP Privacy Notice Checklist

Audience: founders, product, ops, marketing · Last reviewed: March 2026

Privacy notices are one of the fastest indicators of whether a team understands its real data practices or is just publishing generic policy copy.

Checklist

  1. Can users understand what data is collected?
  2. Is the purpose of collection explained plainly?
  3. Does the notice match product and marketing reality?
  4. Has the notice been reviewed after workflow changes?

What teams usually miss

Support, CRM, analytics, and downstream marketing usage often drift outside what the notice seems to imply.
