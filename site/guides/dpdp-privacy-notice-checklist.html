DPDP Privacy Notice Checklist

Last reviewed: March 2026 · Informational only, not legal advice

Privacy notices are one of the fastest places to spot whether a team understands its data practices or is just decorating them.

Who this matters for

Founders, product teams, ops leads, marketers, and anyone responsible for publishing customer-facing notices.

Checklist

  1. Can users understand what data is being collected?
  2. Is the purpose of collection explained plainly?
  3. Does the notice match what the product actually does?
  4. Are downstream use cases reviewed, not assumed?
  5. Has the team revisited the notice after product changes?

Common mistakes

  • Copy-pasting generic policy language
  • Publishing notices nobody internally can explain
  • Letting product behavior drift away from the notice
