How to Map Personal Data

Last reviewed: March 2026 · Informational only, not legal advice

You cannot govern what you cannot see. Data mapping is usually where startup teams discover how much their reality differs from their assumptions.

What to map

  • What data you collect
  • Where it enters the business
  • Who can access it
  • Where it is stored
  • Why it is used

Practical steps

  1. Start with user/account creation flows
  2. Review forms, product events, CRM, support, and analytics systems
  3. List key vendors and tools touching personal data
  4. Document obvious retention and deletion gaps
