About

Built for practical implementation, not legal theater

dpdpact.info exists to make India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act easier to understand and operationalize for founders, startup teams, SaaS operators, agencies, and implementation-minded businesses.

What this site tries to do well

  • Explain complex topics in plain English
  • Focus on operational usefulness
  • Provide starter resources and checklists
  • Help teams move from vague awareness to practical action

What this site is not

  • Not a substitute for legal advice
  • Not a law-firm style publication
  • Not built for performative compliance content

Editorial stance

We care about clarity, implementation, and helping teams ask better operational questions. High-risk or business-critical interpretation should still be checked with qualified legal professionals.
