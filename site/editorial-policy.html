Editorial Policy

dpdpact.info is built to provide plain-English, implementation-oriented DPDP guidance for founders, operators, and teams that need practical help, not legal theater.

Source approach

  • Prefer official/legal/government materials first
  • Use secondary commentary as support, not as primary authority
  • Distinguish between settled law, draft materials, sector overlays, and commentary

Style approach

  • Plain English over jargon
  • Workflow relevance over abstract restatement
  • Informational, not legal advice
